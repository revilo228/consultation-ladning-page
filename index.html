Why horoscopes never fit

Your star sign is probably wrong.

The star signs in apps and magazines are fixed to the seasons. The seasons have drifted about 24 degrees away from the stars.

Vedic astrology uses where the planets actually are. For most people that moves their sun sign back by one. It's often why horoscopes never felt like you.

And it does something Western astrology can't: it times your life. It shows the years that tested you, the ones that are coming, and what each one is for.

The real problem

Working twice as hard and moving half as fast?

You've tried more discipline, more books, more positive thinking. Maybe therapy, manifestation or tarot. Each helps with something. None of them tell you when.

It's 1am. You've rewritten the same email four times. Quit, stay, launch, wait. You still haven't pressed send.

"It feels like there's a wall in front of me, and pushing harder doesn't move it."

Sometimes the wall isn't you. It's the period you're in. A period built for stripping back fights every attempt to expand. A period built for growth makes the same effort go further.

Pushing hard in the wrong period is like driving with the handbrake on. Your reading shows which period you're in. It shows when it ends, and what to do until then.

A reading doesn't replace therapy or medical, legal or financial advice. It answers the timing question they can't.

Before you book

I'm sceptical of astrology. Is this for me?

Sceptics make good clients. You don't need to believe anything. Judge the reading on the years you've already lived. If it can't describe those, don't trust what it says about the years ahead.

How is Vedic astrology different from my star sign?

Western star signs are fixed to the seasons. The seasons have drifted about 24 degrees from the stars. Vedic astrology uses where the planets actually are, so most people's sun sign moves back by one. It also uses dashas, the periods your life runs in. That's why it can talk about timing, not just personality.

Is this just going to tell me what I want to hear?

No. I prepare your chart before we speak, and I tell you what it shows, including when the answer is "not yet".

Does this mean I have no free will?

No. Think of it like a weather forecast. You can't choose the weather, but you can choose what you do in it. The reading shows the weather and what to do about it.
